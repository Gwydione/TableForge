TableForge privacy

- Your data stays on your computer. TableForge keeps your collections, tables, roll history and settings in its data
  folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\TableForge; "Open Data Folder" at the bottom of the window shows it). There is no cloud sync
  and no TableForge account.
- No telemetry. TableForge does not collect analytics, usage statistics or crash reports, and does not contact any
  TableForge server.
- dddice is optional. Only if you choose dddice dice does TableForge connect to the dddice service (dddice.com) and load
  its dice script and models from dddice's servers. It sends only what the dice need: the kind of dice rolled, a guest
  or account login, a room named "TableForge" and the rolls made in it. Table names, table text and results are never sent.
  dddice's own privacy policy applies to its service.
- TableForge never asks for or stores your dddice password. If you connect a dddice account, you approve the connection
  on dddice.com in your own browser. TableForge then keeps dddice's access token in dddice-account.json, encrypted with
  Windows (DPAPI) so that only your Windows user account can read it; Disconnect deletes it.
- The Streaming Overlay is optional and off by default. While it is on, TableForge lets programs on your own computer (such as
  OBS) read the current result (table name, roll value and result text) at 127.0.0.1; it is never reachable from the network
  and sends nothing anywhere. For dddice's dice in OBS, you copy dddice's own streaming link on dddice.com; TableForge never
  sees or stores it.
- Copy Table Text puts the table's text on the Windows clipboard, where other programs on your computer can read it, as
  with any copy.
- Uninstalling TableForge removes the program but leaves your data folder, so your tables are still there if you
  reinstall. Delete that folder yourself to remove them.
